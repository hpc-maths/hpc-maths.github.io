.. title:: Résumé de l'exposé

This summer, the HPC@Maths team participated to the 2025 NASA Modelling Summer Visit at NASA Ames through 5 projects on :

- two-phase flows

- plasma models

- rarefied flows

- code coupling

- IMEX and MR schemes


During this seminar, for each project, a team member will give an overview of the project's objectives, the achievements of the summer program and some perspectives.
