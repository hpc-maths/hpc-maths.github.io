.. title:: Résumé de l'exposé

This seminar presents recent progress in high-fidelity modeling and observations of aircraft contrail formation. 
The work combines large-eddy simulations with Lagrangian particle microphysics to study contrails across the jet and vortex regimes, 
including the transition from early exhaust plume mixing to wake-vortex evolution. 
We investigate how atmospheric and aircraft-engine parameters influence ice crystal formation and radiative forcing, 
with comparisons between conventional Jet A and hydrogen-fueled aircraft. 
The seminar also discusses efforts to connect simulations with ground-based camera observations and flight/meteorological data, 
highlighting current challenges in predicting contrail formation.
