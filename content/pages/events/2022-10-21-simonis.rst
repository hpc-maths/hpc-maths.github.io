.. title:: Titre

Lattice Boltzmann methods (LBM) promise an unconventional, perfectly
parallel approximation technique for partial differential equations appearing
in computational fluid dynamics and beyond. Although the method's intrinsic
connection of relaxation and discretization limits promotes a simplistic
implementation, it also complicates its numerical analysis. In this talk, we
present novel techniques for the latter. We discuss a constructive ansatz
for LBM, the indication of non-linear stability with high performance
computing machinery, and limit consistent discretizations.
