.. title:: Résumé de l'exposé

Fast-transient events are phenomena characterized by significant pressure variations that induce substantial mechanical loads. 
Most of these events can occur in both single- and two-phasecompressible flows within piping systems. 
The water-hammer phenomenon is one such example. Traditionally, one-dimensional approaches involving cross-sectional averaging 
are used to model these events. A general framework is presented here for deriving and analyzing the associated governing equations. 
Particular attention is paid to the area-averaged Euler and Baer-Nunziato equations in elastic pipes. 
The main results of the mathematical analysis of the two corresponding systems are summarized. 
Furthermore, a Finite-Volume framework for the approximation of these equations, 
based on the integral form of their three-dimensional counterpart, is discussed, along with some numerical results.
Work in collaboration with Ray A. Berry and Pascal Galon.
