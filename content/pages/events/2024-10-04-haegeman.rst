.. title:: Résumé de l'exposé

In this presentation, we will discuss the modelling of conservation laws. A generic framework is presented. It consists in two steps: first, a reversible model is obtained through Stationary Action Principle and second, dissipation is added following irreversible thermodynamics. The methodology is applied to two cases: the derivation of a multi-fluid model including relative velocity and turbulent agitation and the derivation of a hyperbolic approximation of the Euler-Fourier model that has recently been proposed [1]. For the second case, we also propose a comparison of the model with kinetic theory.

[1] Dhaouadi F., Garvilyuk S., An Eulerian hyperbolic model for heat transfer derived via Hamilton’s principle: analytical and numerical study. Proc. R. Soc. A. 2024
